# First prospect hypothesis

## Candidate

**Sofie Pavitt Face** — [sofiepavittface.com](https://www.sofiepavittface.com/)

This is a prospect to validate, not a customer or an endorsement. Its public
site presents an acne-focused skincare range and a three-step system bundle,
so a brief tracking acne-care peers' product positioning and visible offers
could be relevant. The public product page checked for the sample snapshot
displayed a bundle price and discount; that alone does not establish a need or
willingness to pay.

## Draft note for the owner to send personally

> Hi Sofie Pavitt Face team — I’m testing a small, human-reviewed competitor
> watch for acne-care skincare brands. I made a short example comparing public
> product-page positioning and bundle details, with source links. It’s only a
> snapshot, not a claim about sales impact. Would a brief like this be useful
> for your team, and which brands or changes would you actually want tracked?
> If not, no worries. I can send the example if helpful.

Review the recipient, current site, and note before sending. Do not automate
outreach or imply the brand asked for the research.

## Validation questions

1. Is competitor-page monitoring useful enough to spend time or budget on?
2. Which 3-5 brands and changes matter most (offers, bundles, launches,
   product-page messaging, or availability)?
3. What reporting cadence and format would fit the team's workflow?
4. Would they pay for a one-time pilot? If yes, agree on scope and price before
   producing custom recurring work.
