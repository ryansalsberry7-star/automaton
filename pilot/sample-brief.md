## What this may suggest

- **Observation:** The Sofie Pavitt page presents a multi-step routine with a
  bundle discount; the Dieux page presents a single moisturizer with a
  barrier-support and makeup-prep story.
- **Hypothesis to test:** A brand may find it useful to see whether peer
  messaging, bundle offers, and displayed availability change from week to
  week.
- **Not established:** This snapshot does not show competitor change over
  time, whether either offer affects sales, or whether the products are
  directly comparable in formulation or use.

## Human review before sharing

- Re-open each page and confirm the visible details and date.
- Remove or correct any price, stock, or review information that has changed.
- Confirm the customer considers these brands relevant peers.
- Keep brand claims attributed and do not turn them into medical advice.
